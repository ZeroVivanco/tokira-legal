# Tokira Privacy Notice

Version 1 · Effective September 30, 2026

## In thirty seconds

- Your patients, medications, treatments, doses and notes are stored **only on your phone**. Tokira has no
  account and no server of its own, and I cannot see them.
- **Only if you agree**, Tokira sends Google crash reports and data about how you use the app. The names of your
  patients, your medications and your notes are never sent. You can turn it off any time in Settings.
- In most countries nothing is sent at all, and the app does not even ask.
- If you want what was sent deleted, or have any question, write to me at **zerovivanco.it@gmail.com**.

## 2. What Tokira does with your data

Tokira is a medication reminder. What you write in it (who your patients are, which medications they take, at
what times, which doses you logged and which notes you left) is stored in your phone's memory and never leaves
it. Tokira asks for no account, has no server of its own and uploads that information nowhere.

That means:

- I have no access to what you record.
- If you change phones, the information does not travel with the app.
- If you uninstall Tokira, the information is deleted with it and cannot be recovered.

## 3. Health data

What you record about medications and doses is information about your health, or that of the people you care
for or your pets. The law treats it as **sensitive personal data**. That is why Tokira handles it in the simplest
way there is: it does not collect it. It stays on your phone and is never sent to Google or anyone.

If you care for someone else, you decide what you record about them. Tokira shares it with nobody.

## 4. What is sent, only if you agree

The first time you use Tokira it asks whether you want to help improve it. Until you answer, **nothing is sent**,
even if the app crashes. You can say yes to everything, no to everything, or choose separately:

### Crash reports

- **What is sent**: the point in the code where the app closed, your phone model, the Android and Tokira versions, the
  available memory, and an installation identifier.
- **What is not sent**: the error message is removed before the report is sent, because it could contain
  something you typed. No names, medications or notes are sent either.
- **What it is for**: to find and fix the bugs.
- **Who receives it**: Google, through its Firebase Crashlytics service.

### Usage data

- **What is sent**: which screens you open; whether reminders arrive on time on your phone; whether you log your doses on
  time, late or snooze them; whether a treatment is for you, for someone else or for a pet; how many patients
  and treatments you have, in ranges ("2 to 3", "4 to 6"); and which system permissions the app has.
- **What is not sent**: never the name of a patient, a medication or a treatment, nor your notes, nor the exact time of a
  dose. Each value travels as a word from a closed list or as a range, never as text you wrote.
- **What it is for**: to learn what works and what does not, and decide what to improve.
- **Who receives it**: Google, through Google Analytics for Firebase.

Bear in mind that, even without saying which medication you take, usage data does say that you use a medication
app and how regularly you log your doses. Out of caution I treat it as if it were health data, which is why it is
only sent with your express consent.

### What Google adds on its own

When you agree, Google Analytics also records when you open the app and for how long, your phone's model and
language, the Android version and an **approximate location** (country and region) inferred from your
connection. Tokira enables none of Google's advertising features, does not use your phone's advertising
identifier and does not link this data to any account.

### Where it is sent from

It is only sent from these countries: Mexico, Colombia, Chile, Panama, the Dominican Republic, Costa Rica,
Paraguay, Guatemala, Honduras, Bolivia, the United States, Puerto Rico and New Zealand. Your phone decides by
itself, from the country of your SIM, your network and your time zone, without asking anyone. Outside those
countries Tokira does not ask and sends nothing.

## 5. On what basis

Only with your **express consent**: the answer you give in the app, recorded on your phone with the version of
this notice, the date and the country. The data that stays on your phone needs no basis, because I do not
process it.

## 6. How to withdraw your consent

In **Settings → Help improve Tokira** there is a switch for crash reports and another for usage data. Turning one
off makes Tokira stop sending that kind of data at once and delete whatever was waiting to be sent on the phone.
Turning both off also retires the installation identifier.

Withdrawing consent does not affect what was already sent. Section 8 is for that.

## 7. How long it is kept

- Crash reports are deleted from Firebase after **90 days**.
- Usage data linked to your identifier is deleted from Google Analytics after **14 months**; aggregated
  totals, which identify nobody, are kept.
- What is on your phone is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## 8. Your rights and how to delete what was sent

You can ask me to tell you what was sent from your installation, to correct it, to delete it or to stop
processing it. As there are no accounts, what was sent is filed under **identifiers** you can see and copy in
**Settings → Privacy**. Write to zerovivanco.it@gmail.com with them; the "Request deletion" option on that same
page writes the email for you.

I answer within **20 business days** at most and, where it applies, carry out your request within the following
15 days. I do not need to know who you are: the identifiers are enough.

For what is on your phone there is no need to ask me: edit or delete it in the app, or uninstall it.

## 9. Transfers

Google processes this data on my behalf and under my instructions, on its servers, which may be in the United
States or other countries. I do not sell data, do not hand it to anyone else and do not use it for advertising.
Google may not use it for its own purposes.

## 10. Security

What is on your phone is as protected as your phone: use a screen lock. What is sent to Google travels
encrypted. No system is infallible; that is why what is sent is the minimum and never carries your text.

## 11. Minors

Tokira is for adults. An adult may record in it the medications of a minor in their care; that information stays
on the phone like everything else.

## 12. Changes to this notice

Each version of this notice has a number and a date, and earlier versions stay published. If a change affects
what is sent, to whom or why, Tokira asks you again inside the app and sends nothing until you answer. Changes of
wording are announced on this page.

## 13. Tokira is not medical advice

Tokira reminds. It does not prescribe, does not review your doses and does not replace a healthcare
professional.
